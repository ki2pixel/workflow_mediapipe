# Academic institutions[](#academic-institutions)

Lightning Studios serve as an ideal platform to organize and manage research efforts across a university or school. It can unify research groups, streamline spend, and equip researchers, lecturers, and students with reproducible and collaborative coding environments.

# Advantages[](#advantages)

Lightning is designed to scale AI across the largest enterprises and universities.

## Unified platform[](#unified-platform)

As AI scales inside a university, complexity and new needs arise. These new needs are patched by integrating a new tool to solve a specialized part of the ML lifecycle. Soon, you have a complex platform that is expensive and difficult to maintain. Most universities do not have the engineering resources to maintain such a system and do not want to pay for dozens of unrelated tools.

Lightning is the first end-to-end AI platform built for enterprise and university scale. All systems are unified in a single Studio interface.


An all-in-one Studio vs. a bunch of disparate tools and services stitched together.

Select an Image

## Instant access to Students[](#instant-access-to-students)

Students can log into Lightning with their .edu emails and automatically access university cloud resources through a simple user interface. Every year, millions of dollars are lost to unclaimed cloud credits or inefficient use of cloud resources.

## Custom cloud deployments[](#custom-cloud-deployments)

Lightning connects your different compute backends into a single, unified system. Run on AWS, GCP and connect your on-prem clusters as well. universities usually have multiple on-prem clusters that can be connected and accessed through a single, unified interface.


Connect cloud and on prem to a single unified system.

## Public and private templates[](#public-and-private-templates)

Create internal collections of Studio templates to reduce time to build, share models, datasets and reproducible AI systems. As a member of the Lightning community, universities have access to publish and use high-performance templates created by the AI community.

Start a new project from a [Studio template](https://lightning.ai/studios) .

## Create university libraries[](#create-university-libraries)

Universities can create libraries of their high-performance Studios to enable the public access to the high-performance models and AI systems produced by universities.
