## Authors submit Studios[](#authors-submit-studios)

During the review process, our suggestion is to encourage paper publishers to submit a Studio with their publication review. This will allow reviewers to validate claims made on the papers, inspect code, results and more. Once a paper has been accepted, conference administrators can choose to feature the published Studios in their conference library for the global AI community to explore and reproduce.


Studios can support review for publishing and bring attention to published papers.

# Advantages[](#advantages)

## Full reproducibility[](#full-reproducibility)

Academia suffers from a reproducibility crisis, where claims made in paper cannot be 100% verified. Studios allow reviewers to duplicate the author's work in <1 minute by having the author published a Studio which is fully self-contained with all code, files, dependency and environment details. Lightning can work with conferences to reduce the cost of doing this.

https://pl-bolts-doc-images.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/Docs\_Studios\_MP4/Academia\_Conference\_Reproducibility3.mp4

Duplicate a Studio to quickly reproduce and review an author's work.

## Transparency[](#transparency)

The scientific community can validate work by duplicating a Studio, inspecting the code and results to verify claims. This dramatically increases transparency and accountability at a time when more and more people are getting into AI.


Reproducing results in a Studio bolsters transparency and accountability in research.

Select an Image

## Accelerate reusability[](#accelerate-reusability)

Published work today sits on GitHub repos that people cannot fully reproduce because they lack all environment details, the type of compute required, the right cuda version, and so on.

Published Studios can instantly be duplicated and integrated as a module into follow up work by anyone in the world.

https://pl-bolts-doc-images.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/Docs\_Studios\_MP4/Academia\_Conference\_PublishGlobally3.mp4

Publish Studios to be globally available for review and reproduction.

## Faster review process[](#faster-review-process)

It is much easier to validate claims by duplicating a Studio in a few minutes instead of spending days or weeks debating the merits of a claim, \(or even if a claim at all can hold\). Many science disciplines that use AI show empirical results as a means to prove claims. The tooling must evolve to enable this new kind of validation. Studios unlock this phase of scientific transparency.


Validate claims in a paper by duplicating the Studio that contains its code and files for reproducibility.
