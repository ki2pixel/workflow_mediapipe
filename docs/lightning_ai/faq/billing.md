# Credits[](#credits)

Users get 1 free 4-CPU Studio to code in and run workloads on. The remaining CPUs and GPUs will be billed as advertised. The first 10 GB of storage in the Drive for free. Credits can be used to scale up compute to large CPUs and GPUs and store production-level datasets within the Lightning platform.

## How am I billed?[](#how-am-i-billed)

There are 2 activities that consume credits, running machines \(in Studios or Jobs\) and storage \(above 10 GB\). Credits are billed per second when running a Studio or Job that is not marked as "Free".

Additionally, the first 10 GB of data in the Drive are unbilled. After that users will be billed at a rate of $0.10 / GB / month \(billed daily\).


Paid Studios are billed per second and data storage is billed daily.

## How do free credits work?[](#how-do-free-credits-work)

For free plans, users are topped up to 15 free credits on the 1st of every month. Free credits can be used on storage or GPUs. Free credits are consumed first, followed by purchased credits. Free coding on 4-CPU Studios are only available in 1 Studio at a time. All subsequent Studios will be billed based on the listed machine prices.

* *Conditions for free credits **

1\. Free credits _do not accumulate_ , they are topped up to 15 on the 1st of the month. So if you use 10 credits, you will be granted 10 more. If you use 5, you will be granted 5 more credits.

2\. Users will only be topped up for _the credits they use_. If you add other users to your Teamspace and they consume your credits, you will not be topped up for that consumption.

## Purchasing credits[](#purchasing-credits)

If purchased, Lightning credits expire after 1 year. For example, a Lightning Pro includes 40 credits per month. These credits expire after 1 year. Renewing a subscription adds another 40 credits.

When credits are running low, Lightning will warn users and then attempt to gracefully shut down all workloads and Studios. To continue using Lightning, either wait for additional free credits or purchase additional credits. Many customers take advantage of signing up for a Pro monthly subscription because it comes with 40 credits and access to much more powerful GPUs and other features\!

# Storage[](#storage)

The first 10 GB of data you save on the Drive are free. After that, they are billed at a rate of $0.10 / GB / month \(billed daily\). Your data connections are not billed. Each tier is subject to a different total limit of data that can be stored in the Drive.

  - Free: 50 GB total storage limit \(only first 10 GB free\)


  - Pro: 200 GB total storage limit \(only first 10 GB free\)


  - Teams: 2 TB total storage limit \(only first 10 GB free\)


  - Enterprise: Unlimited storage \(only first 10 GB free\)
